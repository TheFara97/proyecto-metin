[Y_PADDING=10;CENTER_ALIGN]*At the Fisherman, you can craft Rod Enhancements using Fisher Stars.
[Y_PADDING=10;CENTER_ALIGN]*When crafted, each Enhancement receives a random amount of uses (10-20).
[COLOR=0x54e674;Y_PADDING=10;CENTER_ALIGN]*Each fishing attempt consumes 1 use. Once all uses are depleted, the Enhancement disappears from the rod.
