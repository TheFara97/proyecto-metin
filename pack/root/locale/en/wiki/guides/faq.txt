[COLOR=0xAFE996]Q: How many books do you need to do the skill P?
A: You need 55 books to do the skill P.

[COLOR=0xAFE996]Q: What is the maximum level on the server?
A: The maximum level that you can be is 120.

[COLOR=0xAFE996]Q: Can I complete Demon's Tower at level 40?
A: No, you require to be level 75 in order to go to the last floors.

[COLOR=0xAFE996]Q: Where do the level 75 weapons drop?
A: The level 75 weapons drop in Demon's Tower and in Grotto 2 Final at the Ice Witch.

[COLOR=0xAFE996]Q: What bonuses do I require for Demon's Tower?
A: You require Strong against Undead on your items.

[COLOR=0xAFE996]Q: What bonuses do I require for Spider Baroness?
A: You require Poison resistance and Strong against animals on your items. 

[COLOR=0xAFE996]Q: What bonuses do I require for Azrael?
A: You require Strong against Vampires on your items.

[COLOR=0xAFE996]Q: What bonuses do I require for Beran Setau?
A: You require Strong against Devils on your items.

[COLOR=0xAFE996]Q: What bonuses do I require for Razador?
A: You require Fire resistance on your items.

[COLOR=0xAFE996]Q: What bonuses do I require for Jotun?
A: You require Strong against Orks on your items.
